Using the logo

Leave clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of its height on every side, so it does not crowd other logos in a sponsor band. Below about 160 pixels or 35 mm wide the tagline stops being readable; at that size it is better to use the square version.

Please do

  • Use the files on this page as they are
  • Scale proportionally, holding Shift when you resize
  • Match the version to the background
  • Give the logo the same visual weight as other partners in a logo row

Please don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate or crop the logo
  • Recolour the letters or the coloured dots
  • Add shadows, outlines or effects
  • Place the full-colour version on a busy photo or a mid-tone background
  • Rebuild the logo or retype the tagline

Naming the network

In running text, write the name out in full the first time and use the short form after that.

Full nameDutch Caribbean Digital Competence Network
Short formDCDC Network, or DCDC
Partner universitiesUniversity of Aruba, University of Curaçao and University of St. Martin
Funding lineThe DCDC Network is funded by NWO through the Open Science NL programme.
Websitedcdc.network
If you are describing the network's part in your event or publication, please use the wording we agreed with you, such as "hosted by" or "in partnership with". Do not present the network as a funder, sponsor or organiser unless that has been agreed in writing.
