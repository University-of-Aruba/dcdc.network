★ Deadline 31 August 2026 Headline call

Remote access to AI and supercomputing, open to all Small Island Developing States

UNESCO and the Computing and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory at the Brazilian Center for Physics Research (AI-Lab/CBPF) have opened a joint call for remote access to artificial intelligence and high-performance computing infrastructure. The LAB-IA facility pairs HPC, machine learning, and scientific instrumentation for research across astrophysics, geophysics, materials science, quantum technologies, and biophysics. The call sits under UNESCO's Remote Access to Lab Equipment initiative and its Recommendation on Open Science.

Every one of the six Dutch Caribbean islands is a Small Island Developing State, which means the whole network is eligible. The call is open to MSc and PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and junior faculty from SIDS, as well as from Latin America and the Caribbean, Africa, and Ukraine. You need a clearly defined research project and strong English. Projects start in October.

Training

R comes to Curaçao and St. Martin this autumn

Introduction to R for SPSS Users, the two-day course that ran for the first time in Aruba this April, travels to the other islands this autumn. Same structure, localised examples, aimed at researchers and students moving off SPSS toward reproducible, open tools.

The Curaçao edition runs the week of 14 September, co-facilitated with Marjorie Alfonso, our coordinator in Curaçao. The St. Martin edition follows in October. Registration details and the optional R installation clinics go out closer to each date; the Training page carries the full schedule as it firms up.

Package update

islandcodes: version 0.2.0 on the way

The network's first CRAN package, islandcodes, featured in Issue 1, keeps the BES islands separable in country-classification joins. Version 0.2.0 has been submitted to CRAN, and a short paper describing the package is queued for the Journal of Open Source Software. Use cases, missing classifications, and edge cases are welcome via GitHub issues or at rendell.dekort@ua.aw.
