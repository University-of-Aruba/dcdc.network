Save the date: DCDC Kick-Off Conference

10 to 12 November 2026, University of Aruba

The DCDC Network meets on Aruba for its Kick-Off Conference: researchers, data stewards, librarians, statisticians and the people in government, NGOs and business who keep and use data across the six islands of the Dutch Caribbean.

  • When Tuesday 10 November, evening, to Thursday 12 November 2026
  • Where University of Aruba, Oranjestad
  • Format An opening evening with speakers, then two days of workshops and discussion

The programme is built from what members tell us in the network survey. The survey is still open, so there is still time to shape what goes on the programme.
