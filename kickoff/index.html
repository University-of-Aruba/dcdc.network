DCDC Kick-Off Conference: registration is open

10 to 12 November 2026, University of Aruba. Sign up before 3 November.

The DCDC Network meets on Aruba to connect researchers, institutions and professionals across the Dutch Caribbean, from Aruba, Curaçao and St. Martin to Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba, who share a commitment to open data, collaborative research and building digital capacity in our region. The kick-off will set what the network focuses on in the period ahead. Anyone interested in data skills and open science is welcome, for the whole conference or part of it.

  • When Tuesday 10 November, evening, to Thursday 12 November 2026
  • Where University of Aruba, Oranjestad
  • Sign up Before Tuesday 3 November

Speakers

Esther Plomp

Welcome, opening evening, Tuesday 10 November, and workshops on Wednesday and Thursday

Dr. Esther Plomp is the Project Lead of the DCDC Network and its local coordinator for Aruba. She works at the University of Aruba Research Center (UARC) on making researchers’ work more accessible through the UA repository, and sees data competency as essential for Aruba to address its most urgent challenges with evidence. She hopes the network becomes a gateway for people to achieve their data competency and open science goals together.

Hadeikaye Peterson

Keynote, opening evening, Tuesday 10 November

Hadeikaye Peterson is a PhD Candidate in Educational Technology and Learning Design at Simon Fraser University, Canada. Her research examines how older adults develop digital competence at work in a Caribbean small island state undergoing digital transformation. Drawing on asset-based pedagogy and recognition theory, she challenges deficit views of adult learners. A former research assistant on SFU's SSHRC-funded New Literacies of Automation project, she designs hands-on training for adults learning new digital tools, including AI.

Sandy Odor

Talk, opening evening, Tuesday 10 November: Open Data Meets Privacy: Understanding Personal Data, Data Protection Principles and Individual Rights

Sandy Odor, LL.B., LL.M., CAMS, is a legal and privacy professional who obtained her Master of Laws (LL.M.) degree from the University of Aruba in 2013. She currently serves as Group Privacy Officer for a banking group operating across the Dutch Caribbean. Her expertise includes data protection compliance, privacy governance, and safeguarding individuals’ rights. She is committed to promoting privacy awareness and responsible data processing across organizations and jurisdictions.

Open data offers valuable opportunities for research, innovation and knowledge sharing, but it also raises important questions about the protection of personal information. This presentation introduces the concept of personal data, explains the fundamental principles of data protection in the Dutch Caribbean, and outlines the rights individuals have regarding the processing of their personal information. The session aims to raise awareness of how personal data can be handled responsibly while supporting research, transparency and open-data initiatives.

Marvin Odor

Workshop: open science in your organisation

Marvin Odor is an instructional designer, IT and media expert, and data collector specialist at the Instituto Pedagógico Arubano (IPA). He brings 17 years in education, including teaching software and design techniques at Colegio EPI. He leads large-scale tablet-based surveys across Aruba’s schools and designs the digital workflows behind them, supporting evidence-based decision-making. Representing IPA and its research team CIDE.
